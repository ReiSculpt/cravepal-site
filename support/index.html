Something not working, or a question? We're two founders and we read every email.

Contact us

Email hello@cravepal.app. Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version if something's broken. Your Diagnostics export (Settings, Diagnostics) helps too, and never names the apps you use.

Cancel or change your subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then CravePal. Or open CravePal, Settings, Manage subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. For a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore a purchase

New iPhone or reinstalled? Open CravePal and tap Restore purchases, under the plans or in Settings. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

The Pause doesn't appear

  • Check Screen Time permission is on: iPhone Settings, Screen Time, and make sure CravePal is allowed.
  • Check CravePal isn't paused, and that it's inside your on guard times (Settings, On guard schedule).
  • Open Settings, Guarded apps, and pick your apps again. iOS updates sometimes forget them.

Let yourself into an app

You're never locked out. Tap "order anyway" on the Pause, or open CravePal and tap Pause CravePal in Settings to switch every Pause off.

Delete your account

Open CravePal, Settings, Account, Delete account. It deletes your account straight away. It doesn't cancel an Apple subscription. Can't get in? Email hello@cravepal.app and we'll do it for you.

Removing CravePal

Tap Pause CravePal in Settings first, then delete the app as usual. Everything CravePal kept on your iPhone goes with it. See our privacy policy and terms of use.
